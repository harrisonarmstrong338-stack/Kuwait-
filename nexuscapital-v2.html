FAQ
Common Questions
Answered.
How do I receive my profits? +
Profits are calculated daily and credited to your VaultFX wallet balance each morning at 08:00 UTC. You can withdraw to your preferred method (BTC, USDT, ETH, bank wire) at any time from your dashboard with no waiting period on active plans.
Is my capital protected? +
Your deposited principal is protected by our Capital Preservation Policy on Starter and Silver plans. Our proprietary risk management never allocates more than 3% of your capital to a single trade, and stop-loss systems automatically close positions to protect your principal balance.
How does the Bitcoin mining work? +
When you invest, a portion is allocated to purchase mining capacity in our industrial ASIC farm. Your allocated TH/s is shown on your dashboard and mines BTC continuously. Rewards from mined blocks are distributed proportionally to all pool participants daily.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether USDT (TRC20 & ERC20), Litecoin, bank wire transfer (SWIFT/SEPA), and major credit/debit cards via Stripe. Crypto deposits confirm in under 10 minutes.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
Yes — plan upgrades are instant. You can top up your balance to meet the next tier's minimum at any time, and the higher ROI rate applies from the next daily profit cycle. There are no penalties, fees, or waiting periods for upgrades.
How does auto-compounding work? +
When compounding is enabled in your dashboard settings, your daily profit is automatically reinvested into your portfolio before the next cycle begins. This means you earn returns on your returns — compounding daily creates exponential growth over time.
